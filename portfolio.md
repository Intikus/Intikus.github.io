# Rain Meadow
## Soundtrack, Plop Machine, Music Synchronization

Joined the moment development started, as the musician for the pacifistic online multiplayer mod for Rain World, with the motive of create transient songs to be randomly played and give a sense of *homelyness*, similar to Might and Delight's "Meadow".

Later, a crash course by Henpemaz steered me to learn C#, concurring with creating a real time procedural music generator in said language. This would be inserted as the "Plop Machine" that perpetually plays when near a regions HUB.

Wrote a music player system that would syncronize your music to other players in your proximity.
